\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\emph{Six energies, one
dataset.}}{Six energies, one dataset.}}\label{six-energies-one-dataset.}

We took our name from Manifest's \emph{``Arıyo''} --- six of us, each
carrying a member's energy into the project.

\begin{quote}
Each of us is paired with one Manifest member whose vibe matches the
role we played in this project. Click the names to visit our personal
EMU430 pages.
\end{quote}

\subsubsection{Işıl Çakır}\label{iux15fux131l-uxe7akux131r}

{Hilal · the leader's poise}

{Project leadCoordination}

Işıl held the project together --- from initial dataset selection
through final-page polish. If something needed deciding, Işıl decided
it; if a deadline was approaching, Işıl was already three days ahead.
Calm, systematic, and impossible to fluster.

\href{https://emu-hacettepe-analytics.github.io/emu430-spring2026-isilcakrr/}{→
isilcakrr's page}

\subsubsection{Tuğçe Başaran}\label{tuux11fuxe7e-baux15faran}

{Lidya · the analyst's edge}

{Data wranglingEDA}

Tuğçe lives in the data. She was the one who first noticed how skewed
the company-tonnage distribution was, and pushed us toward the log-scale
density plot you'll see in the Analysis page. Sharp, precise, and never
satisfied with a chart that's only \emph{almost} right.

\href{https://emu-hacettepe-analytics.github.io/emu430-spring2026-TugceBasaran/}{→
TugceBasaran's page}

\subsubsection{Zeynep Kızkapan}\label{zeynep-kux131zkapan}

{Mina · the storyteller}

{NarrativeWriting}

Zeynep turned numbers into a story. The narrative voice across these
pages --- and the framing of the 20,000-ton question as a real policy
question rather than just a stat --- comes from her. Warm, clear, and
quietly persuasive.

\href{https://emu-hacettepe-analytics.github.io/emu430-spring2026-zeynepkizkapann/}{→
zeynepkizkapann's page}

\subsubsection{Selin Atmaca}\label{selin-atmaca}

{Sueda · the visual instinct}

{DesignVisualization}

Selin is why the site looks like \emph{this} and not like another stock
template. The colour palette, the gradients, the cyan pop you keep
noticing on hover --- that's her hand. Strong taste, gentle delivery.

\href{https://emu-hacettepe-analytics.github.io/emu430-spring2026-selinatmaca-lab/}{→
selinatmaca-lab's page}

\subsubsection{Tarık Buğra Birinci}\label{tarux131k-buux11fra-birinci}

{Esin · the inspiration}

{EngineeringR / Quarto}

Tarık is our engineer. The choropleth, the data-cleaning script, the R
chunks that turn an unruly pivot table into a tidy data frame --- all
his terrain. Whatever the toolchain throws at us, he picks it up and
makes it work.

\href{https://emu-hacettepe-analytics.github.io/emu430-spring2026-birincitarikbugra/}{→
birincitarikbugra's page}

{ModelingTax simulation}
